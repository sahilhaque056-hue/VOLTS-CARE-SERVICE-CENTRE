About Wajid AC Work

Wajid AC Work is a service-focused business providing AC and home-appliance maintenance solutions.

Our services include AC servicing, repair, installation, uninstallation, cooling checks, deep cleaning and water-leakage related service.

We also provide service support for appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines and air coolers.

Customers can contact us directly through phone or WhatsApp to discuss their service requirements and arrange a booking.
